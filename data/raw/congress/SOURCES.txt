members.csv
  Every member of the House and Senate, 1st-119th Congresses, trimmed from Voteview's member file (HSall_members.csv, downloaded 2026-11-20):
  Lewis, Jeffrey B., Keith Poole, Howard Rosenthal, Adam Boche, Aaron Rudkin, and Luke Sonnet. Voteview: Congressional Roll-Call Votes Database.
  https://voteview.com/data   Columns kept: congress, chamber, state, district, party code, occupancy, ICPSR id, name, number of votes.
divisions.json
  Party divisions at the start of each Congress (Office of the House Historian, Senate Historical Office); the 120th is members-elect (see its "elect" block).
assets/districts/NNN.json (built by data/build_districts.py)
  House district boundaries for every Congress: Jeffrey B. Lewis, Brandon DeVine, Lincoln Pitcher and Kenneth C. Martis,
  Digital Boundary Definitions of United States Congressional Districts, 1789-2012 (UCLA), kept current through the 119th Congress,
  https://github.com/JeffreyBLewis/congressional-district-boundaries (MIT License), GeoJson folder downloaded 2026-11-20.
  Simplified with mapshaper (-simplify interval=1000 keep-shapes) before building; the 1.6 GB originals are not kept in this repo.
../elections/house_1976_2024.csv
  MIT Election Data and Science Lab, "U.S. House 1976-2024", Harvard Dataverse, doi:10.7910/DVN/IG0UN2 (CC0), version 20250910.
  Downloaded 2026-11-21 as tab-delimited; rows were wrapped in quotes with escaped inner quotes, so it was unwrapped to plain CSV.
