state_series.json (extracted 2026-11-21)
  debt: State government debt outstanding at the end of each fiscal year, in thousands of dollars, FY1992-2023.
    U.S. Census Bureau, Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances (summary tables, "State" column of "Debt outstanding"),
    https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances/tables/ ; FY2001, FY2003 and FY2023 from the Annual Survey of State
    Government Finances ("Debt at end of fiscal year"), https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/state/tables/ .
    Missing: VT 1996, MT and WV 1999 (not in the published tables). Sharp one-year changes can reflect how Census classifies
    state agencies and authorities (e.g. ID, IN, MO in 2022), not only borrowing or repayment.
  gas: Average motor gasoline price, all grades and sectors, including taxes, dollars per gallon, 1970-2024.
    U.S. Energy Information Administration, State Energy Data System (SEDS), series MGTCD ($/million Btu) x MGTCK (million Btu
    per barrel, U.S. factor when no state factor) / 42. https://www.eia.gov/state/seds/
  elec: Average residential electricity price, cents per kilowatt-hour, 1970-2024. EIA SEDS series ESRCD ($/million Btu) x 0.3412.
  pop: Resident population, 1960-2024. EIA SEDS series TPOPP (from Census Bureau estimates).
