executive_orders.json
  Every executive order with number, signing date, title, source link, status and topic tags, trimmed from
  the Executive Orders Archive (github.com/alinuxfan/executive-orders-archive; executiveordersarchive.org),
  which compiles The American Presidency Project (UC Santa Barbara) and the Federal Register. Complete from
  1929 (Hoover) on; earlier years are partial because early orders were not numbered or collected
  consistently. Topic tags are the archive's automated classification. Snapshot synced 2026-09-25.
