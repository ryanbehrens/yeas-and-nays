Barack Obama

DemocraticVice president: Joe BidenCompleted two terms

Took office in the worst recession since the 1930s, passed the Affordable Care Act and Dodd–Frank, and ordered the raid that killed Osama bin Laden. The debt nearly doubled as recession spending and lower tax revenue pushed deficits past $1 trillion a year.

Notable orders

Ending 'enhanced interrogation'EO 13491January 22, 2009

Limited all interrogations to Army Field Manual techniques, ending the CIA program that used waterboarding.

Federal Register
Closing Guantanamo BayEO 13492January 22, 2009

Ordered the detention camp closed within a year. Congress blocked transfers and it was never closed.

Classified national security informationEO 13526December 29, 2009

Abortion funding and the ACAEO 13535March 24, 2010

Pardons and commutations

A pardon wipes out the punishment for a federal crime; a commutation shortens a sentence but leaves the conviction. Counts are by federal fiscal year. Most commutations came through a program for nonviolent drug offenders serving long sentences under older laws.

IRS targeting of conservative groupsProven2010–2013

The IRS gave extra scrutiny to groups with 'Tea Party' or 'patriot' in their names when they applied for tax-exempt status.

Outcome: Inspector general confirmed it; the IRS apologized and settled lawsuits in 2017; no criminal charges.

Source
'Fast and Furious' gun-walkingProven2009–2011

A federal ATF operation let about 2,000 guns reach Mexican cartels in order to track them. Two were found where Border Patrol agent Brian Terry was killed.

Outcome: The inspector general faulted 14 officials; the attorney general was held in contempt of Congress.

Source
Veterans Affairs wait-time cover-upProven2014

VA hospitals kept secret waiting lists to hide long delays for care; some veterans died waiting.

Outcome: The VA secretary resigned; Congress passed a reform law.

Source
Seizing reporters' phone recordsProven2013

In leak investigations, the Justice Department secretly obtained Associated Press phone records and named a Fox News reporter as a possible co-conspirator in a search warrant.

Outcome: New Justice Department rules on media subpoenas.

Source
Benghazi attackDisputed2012–2016

Four Americans, including the U.S. ambassador, were killed at U.S. facilities in Libya. Critics said security was inadequate and the administration's first explanation was misleading.

Outcome: Eight congressional investigations found security failures; none found intentional wrongdoing by senior officials.

Source
Mass surveillance revealedProven2013

Leaks by Edward Snowden showed the NSA was collecting records of nearly all Americans' phone calls.

Outcome: A federal appeals court later ruled the program unlawful; Congress ended bulk collection in 2015.

Source
Drone strike killing a U.S. citizenDisputed2011

A U.S. strike in Yemen killed Anwar al-Awlaki, an American citizen and al-Qaeda leader, without a trial.

Outcome: Justified by a secret Justice Department memo later made public.

Source
Hillary Clinton's private email serverProven2009–2016

His secretary of state used a private email server, including for some classified information.

Outcome: The FBI called it 'extremely careless' but recommended no charges (2016).
