John F. Kennedy

DemocraticVice president: Lyndon B. JohnsonAssassinated November 22, 1963

The youngest person elected president. His 1,036 days in office included the failed Bay of Pigs invasion, the Cuban Missile Crisis, the start of the race to the moon and a growing U.S. role in Vietnam. He was assassinated in Dallas on November 22, 1963.

'Affirmative action' for contractorsEO 10925March 6, 1961

Bay of Pigs invasionProvenApril 1961

A CIA-trained force of Cuban exiles invaded Cuba and was defeated within three days.

Outcome: Kennedy took public responsibility; more than 100 invaders were killed and about 1,200 captured.

Source
Plots to kill Fidel CastroProven1961–1963

The CIA, working partly with Mafia figures, plotted to assassinate Cuba's leader under 'Operation Mongoose'.

Outcome: Revealed by the Church Committee in 1975; whether Kennedy personally approved the plots was never established.

Source
Wiretapping Martin Luther King Jr.Proven1963

The Justice Department authorized FBI wiretaps on King, which the FBI then used to try to discredit him.

Outcome: Documented by the Church Committee in 1976.

Source
Affair linked to a Mafia bossProven1960–1962

Kennedy had an affair with Judith Campbell Exner, who was also involved with Chicago mob leader Sam Giancana.

Outcome: Documented by the Church Committee; FBI Director Hoover warned Kennedy in 1962.

Source
Hiding his healthProven1960–1963

Kennedy concealed Addison's disease and serious back problems treated with heavy medication, while presenting an image of vigor.

Outcome: Confirmed when his medical records were opened in 2002.
